# Subhasto — live shopping first

Home now leads with one featured live broadcast and a direct room entry, followed by simple rows for other broadcasts, upcoming shows and a short community shop preview. The main navigation keeps live shopping, live-only browsing, Shop and followed sellers; auctions, digital items and scheduled shows remain in filters. Home recommendations continue to respect hidden listings and sellers.

The room gives video, the current item and its primary action more space. Reactions, sound, wallet, sharing and moderator chat filters open through More. Product status, timer, leader, custom bid and buyer protection remain available. The room's Shop contains separate products and the auction queue. Separate shop purchases use the existing confirmation flow; products already assigned to an active live or auction are excluded from separate shop offers.

Profile shows real shop products before account management. The public seller profile now opens its Shop by default: the items screen previously existed but its tab was missing. Auctions, reviews, seller information, messaging and following remain accessible. Product lists use flat photo rows. Search text is held by a controller so a lazy sliver cannot discard it when scrolling.

No payment backend, verification requirements, saved-card storage, seller persistence or Seller Hub routes were changed. Purchase eligibility and successful payment remain server decisions.

Before these edits, commit `208b11ed1f82d2fa98554c53875a79081499434f` was saved as branch `backup/subhasto-before-live-shop-2026-11-04` and a source archive. Existing published archives remain in the Pages workflow.

Validation includes full Flutter analysis and tests, new navigation coverage for public/own shops, narrow displays and 150% text, live entry and hidden tools, and shop inventory/room preservation. The isolated browser fixture also covers live viewers, hosts and seller shops. Test fixtures perform no real payments and do not start a real camera stream.
